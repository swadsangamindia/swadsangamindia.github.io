Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Khata Bahi, privacy is an important consideration because the platform can be used to maintain business and transaction records.

This Privacy Policy explains what types of information may be processed, how information may be stored, what third-party services may be involved and what responsibilities users have when using the service.

By using Khata Bahi, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Khata Bahi and its website-based services accessible through the Khata Bahi web platform.

It covers information associated with use of the platform, including information that users voluntarily enter into business records.

2. Information Users May Enter

Depending on the features used, users may enter information including:

  • Business name
  • Company name
  • GSTIN
  • Phone number
  • Business address
  • Customer names
  • Vendor names
  • Transaction descriptions
  • Dates
  • Amounts
  • Quantities
  • GST information
  • Payment modes
  • Categories
  • Invoice information
  • Credit information
  • Other bookkeeping information

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate authority to enter and process information belonging to other individuals or organizations.

3. Account Information

Some functionality may require users to authenticate through a supported account.

The current website provides a Google Login option.

When authentication is used, account-related information may be processed by the authentication provider.

Users should review the provider's applicable privacy documentation.

4. Local Storage

The existing Khata Bahi privacy information states that transaction ledgers, customer credit entries and Gullak savings entries may be stored locally on the user's device using browser localStorage.

Information stored locally may remain on the device until the user or browser removes it, subject to browser behavior.

Clearing browser storage can potentially remove locally stored information.

Users should therefore maintain appropriate backups of important records.

5. Cloud Storage

Where account synchronization is enabled, information may be synchronized with Google Firebase/Firestore under the authenticated account.

The purpose of synchronization is to provide supported account-based access and data persistence.

The availability and operation of cloud synchronization may depend on third-party infrastructure.

6. Financial Records

Khata Bahi may process business-related records that users choose to enter.

These records may include transaction amounts, customer information, credit information, GST details and invoice information.

Users should consider the sensitivity of information before entering it.

The published privacy policy states that Khata Bahi does not sell or distribute users' financial ledgers.

7. Advertising

The platform may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

The existing privacy notice states that third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on prior visits to the website or other websites.

Advertising technologies may collect or process information according to the relevant third-party provider's policies.

8. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Supporting website functionality
  • Remembering certain settings
  • Authentication-related functionality
  • Advertising
  • Measuring or understanding website usage

Users may be able to control cookies through browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

9. Third-Party Services

Khata Bahi may rely on third-party providers for infrastructure or functionality.

Potential third-party services include:

  • Google authentication
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Firestore
  • Google AdSense
  • Hosting infrastructure
  • Payment services
  • Other technical providers

Each provider may process information under its own policies and terms.

10. Why Information Is Processed

Information may be processed to:

  • Provide bookkeeping functionality
  • Save transactions
  • Synchronize account data
  • Generate invoices
  • Calculate amounts
  • Provide document-conversion functionality
  • Process payments
  • Verify Pro subscriptions
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain platform security
  • Improve functionality
  • Display advertisements
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

11. User Responsibility

Users should not enter personal or confidential information unless they have an appropriate reason and authority to do so.

For example, if a user enters a customer's name and phone number, the user should ensure that collecting and using that information is appropriate for the user's business and legal obligations.

12. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures may be used to protect information handled by the service.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should use strong account-security practices and keep their devices protected.

13. Account Security

Users are responsible for protecting access to their accounts.

If a user's Google account is compromised, unauthorized persons may potentially obtain access to services connected to that account.

Users should use appropriate security features offered by their authentication provider.

14. Data Retention

Information may remain stored for as long as necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain records, comply with legal requirements or support legitimate operational purposes.

Local data may remain until deleted by the user or browser.

Cloud-synchronized information may be retained according to the platform's operational and technical requirements.

15. Data Deletion

Users who want information removed should contact Khata Bahi where the relevant information is maintained by the service.

For locally stored information, users may also need to remove the information from their browser/device.

Where information is maintained through a third-party service, deletion may also be subject to that provider's systems and policies.

16. Children's Privacy

Khata Bahi is designed as a business bookkeeping utility rather than a service directed toward children.

Users should not use the service to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

17. Payment Information

Where users purchase Pro functionality, payment processing may involve a third-party payment mechanism.

Khata Bahi may receive transaction-related information needed to identify or verify a payment.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive financial information through email or support channels.

18. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies.

Advertising providers may use information about browsing activity according to their own policies.

Users can review advertising and privacy controls provided by the relevant advertising provider and browser.

19. External Websites

Khata Bahi may contain links to external websites.

This Privacy Policy does not automatically apply to those websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing information.

20. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Changes may be made because of:

  • New features
  • Technical changes
  • Changes in third-party services
  • Changes in applicable law
  • Changes in data practices

The updated policy will be published on the website.

21. Accuracy

Users are responsible for ensuring that information they provide is accurate and current where accuracy is required.
