About Khata Bahi

Platform Overview and Vision

Khata Bahi is a digital bookkeeping and business-management utility created to make everyday record keeping simpler, more organized and more accessible.

Small businesses often manage a large amount of information every day. A shopkeeper may need to remember customer payments, supplier dues, sales, expenses, receipts, GST amounts, invoice details and outstanding balances. Traditionally, much of this information may be maintained in paper notebooks, spreadsheets or separate applications.

Khata Bahi brings several of these everyday activities together in one digital platform.

Our objective is straightforward: make business record keeping easier without making the process unnecessarily complicated.

The platform is designed with practical everyday business use in mind.

Our Purpose

Khata Bahi was developed around a simple idea: business owners should be able to record financial transactions quickly and understand their basic business records without having to manage complicated software.

The platform is intended particularly for:

  • Shopkeepers
  • Kirana stores
  • Traders
  • Small-business owners
  • Freelancers
  • Independent professionals
  • Service providers
  • Small teams
  • Individuals maintaining business records

The platform provides tools for recording transactions, managing companies, calculating amounts and creating useful documents.

What Khata Bahi Provides

Khata Bahi currently combines bookkeeping functionality with document utilities and business calculators.

The website includes a ledger dashboard, transaction history, company profiles and tools for PDF and Excel documents.

Users can create transaction entries and record information such as the date, description, amount, party name, quantity, GST rate, category and payment mode.

This allows a business owner to maintain a digital record rather than depending entirely on handwritten notebooks.

Digital Ledger

The heart of Khata Bahi is the digital ledger.

A ledger allows users to record transactions in a structured format.

Instead of searching through pages of handwritten information, users can search transaction history electronically.

This can be particularly useful when a business has many customers, suppliers or daily transactions.

Multiple Business Profiles

Businesses are not always limited to one activity.

A person may operate more than one shop, business or project.

Khata Bahi supports company/business profiles so that users can organize information separately where the applicable plan supports the feature.

Separating records can help reduce confusion between different businesses.

Document Utilities

Business owners frequently receive documents in different formats.

A supplier may send a PDF. A customer may send an image. A spreadsheet may need to be converted into a printable document.

Khata Bahi therefore includes document utilities for converting certain PDF, image and Excel files.

The website describes these tools as client-side document-processing utilities.

GST Assistance

GST can become an important part of business record keeping.

Khata Bahi includes GST-related calculation tools intended to make basic calculations easier.

The platform can help users calculate GST amounts and split applicable CGST and SGST components based on the information supplied.

However, Khata Bahi is designed as a calculation and record-keeping utility rather than a replacement for professional tax advice.

Interest Calculation

Credit transactions are common in many businesses.

Khata Bahi provides an interest calculator where users can enter a principal amount, monthly interest rate and duration.

The purpose is to simplify arithmetic and provide a quick calculation.

Users should independently determine whether the rate and arrangement they use are legally and contractually appropriate.

Simplicity

A major principle behind Khata Bahi is simplicity.

A business application does not need to overwhelm the user with hundreds of complicated menus.

The platform therefore focuses on practical functions such as:

Record → Calculate → Review → Export.

This approach is intended to make common tasks easier to understand.

Built for Everyday Business

Khata Bahi is not designed only for large companies.

Small businesses often have different needs from large enterprises.

A local shop may need a simple record of money received and money paid.

A freelancer may need to track clients.

A trader may need customer and supplier records.

A small service provider may need invoices.

The platform attempts to provide useful digital tools for these situations.

Our Approach to Privacy

Privacy is an important part of a bookkeeping product because users may enter commercially sensitive information.

The published privacy information states that transaction ledgers, customer credit entries and Gullak savings entries may be stored locally or synchronized through Google Firebase/Firestore under an authenticated account, and states that financial ledgers are not sold or distributed.

Users should nevertheless avoid entering information they are not authorized to store and should protect their account and devices.

Technology

Khata Bahi uses modern web technologies to provide an accessible browser-based experience.

The goal is to reduce installation barriers and make the service usable through a web browser.

Cloud services may be used for selected account and synchronization functionality.

Continuous Improvement

Khata Bahi is a developing platform.

As users interact with the application, new requirements and practical problems become easier to identify.

Future improvements may include enhanced reporting, better document processing, improved search, additional business utilities, interface improvements and additional account functionality.

Our Users

The most important part of Khata Bahi is the people who use it.

Every business has its own workflow.

Some businesses record transactions daily.

Others may enter information weekly.

Some users need only a basic ledger.

Others may need invoices, calculations and multiple company profiles.

Khata Bahi aims to provide flexible tools that can accommodate these different workflows.

Responsible Use

Technology can make bookkeeping easier, but it cannot replace responsible financial management.

Users should review transactions, maintain backups, verify calculations and seek professional advice where required.

Khata Bahi's role is to provide tools that support these activities.

Our Vision

Our long-term vision is to make practical business-management technology easier to access for small businesses and independent workers.

We believe digital bookkeeping should not require extensive technical knowledge.

A user should be able to open the application, understand the basic interface and begin recording business activity.
