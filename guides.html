Khata Bahi — Complete User Guide

Standard Operating Procedures & Feature Instructions

Welcome to Khata Bahi

Khata Bahi is a digital bookkeeping and business utility platform designed to help users maintain business records, manage transactions, calculate amounts and work with common business documents.

The website provides a dashboard with ledger management, transaction entry, company profiles, document utilities, calculators, account functionality and support information.

This guide explains how to use the major features.

1. Getting Started

Open the Khata Bahi website in a supported modern browser.

The home screen provides access to the main areas of the application.

Depending on the current version, the navigation may include:

  • Home
  • Features
  • Ledger
  • Dashboard
  • Add Entry
  • All Entries
  • PDF & Business Tools
  • Interest & Tax Calculator
  • Company Profile

Begin by reviewing the dashboard.

2. Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard is the central area where you can review your business records.

It may display information such as:

  • Net balance
  • Total receipts
  • Total payments
  • Total sales
  • GST liability
  • Recent transactions
  • Cash-flow information
  • Category information

The values displayed depend on the transactions entered into the active company.

If there are no transactions, the dashboard may show zero values.

3. Create a Company

If you are using Khata Bahi for a business, create a company or business profile.

Enter information such as:

Company/Business Name: Enter the name under which you want the business records maintained.

GSTIN: If applicable, enter the business GSTIN.

Phone Number: Enter the appropriate business contact number.

Business Address: Enter the business address that should appear on supported documents.

Review the information before saving.

4. Multiple Companies

If your plan supports multiple companies, you can create separate business profiles.

For example:

  • Main Shop
  • Second Shop
  • Wholesale Business
  • Freelance Business
  • Family Business

Before recording a transaction, always check which company is currently active.

This helps prevent transactions from being entered under the wrong business.

5. Add a Transaction

To record a transaction, open Add Entry.

Depending on the available version, you may be asked for:

  • Entry type
  • Date
  • Description
  • Amount
  • Party name
  • Quantity
  • GST rate
  • Category
  • Payment mode

Enter the information carefully.

For example:

  • Party: Rahul
  • Description: Grocery purchase
  • Quantity: 10
  • Amount: ₹5,000
  • GST: Applicable rate
  • Payment Mode: UPI

Review the calculated subtotal, GST amount and total before saving.

6. Jama and Udhar

Traditional Indian bookkeeping often uses terms such as Jama and Udhar.

Khata Bahi can be used to record money coming into and going out of the business according to the available transaction options.

Users should choose the correct entry type for each transaction.

If you are uncertain about the accounting treatment of a transaction, consult an accountant.

7. Party Names

Party names help you identify customers, suppliers and other transaction parties.

Use consistent names.

For example, avoid entering the same person as:

  • Rahul
  • Rahul Kumar
  • R. Kumar
  • Rahul K.

unless these are genuinely different parties.

Consistent naming makes searches and record management easier.

8. Search Transactions

The transaction-history section allows users to review previously entered records.

Search using relevant information such as:

  • Party name
  • Description
  • Transaction information

This can be useful when trying to locate a particular transaction.

9. Reviewing Transactions

Regularly review your entries.

Check:

  • Date
  • Amount
  • Party
  • Description
  • GST
  • Category
  • Payment method

Correct mistakes as soon as possible.

Do not rely on an automatically calculated result without checking the underlying transaction.

10. Exporting Records

Khata Bahi may provide CSV export functionality.

Exported data can be useful for:

  • Backup
  • Spreadsheet analysis
  • Sharing with an accountant
  • Business reporting
  • Record keeping

After exporting, open the resulting file and check that the information has been transferred correctly.

11. PDF to Excel

The PDF-to-Excel tool is designed to extract tables from supported PDF documents into spreadsheet format.

To use it:

  1. Open PDF & Business Tools.
  2. Select PDF-to-Excel.
  3. Choose the PDF.
  4. Start conversion.
  5. Wait for processing.
  6. Download/save the resulting spreadsheet.
  7. Review the output.

Complex PDFs may not convert perfectly.

Always check important financial documents manually.

12. Image to PDF

The Image-to-PDF feature can convert JPG or PNG images into PDF documents.

This may be useful for:

  • Receipts
  • Bills
  • Scanned documents
  • Photographs of documents
  • Records

Select the image and start the conversion.

Review the generated PDF before sharing it.

13. Excel to PDF

The Excel-to-PDF tool can convert supported spreadsheet files into PDF format.

This may be useful when a spreadsheet needs to be printed or shared as a fixed document.

After conversion, review:

  • Page breaks
  • Tables
  • Text
  • Numbers
  • Column alignment

14. PDF to PNG

The platform may provide a PDF-to-PNG utility for rendering a PDF page as an image.

This can be useful when you need an image version of a document page.

Review the output resolution before using it for printing.

15. Interest Calculator

Khata Bahi provides a credit-due interest calculator.

Enter:

  • Principal amount
  • Interest rate per month
  • Number of months

For example, if a user enters a principal of ₹10,000, a monthly rate of 2% and a duration of 3 months, the calculator can provide the corresponding computational result.

The exact calculation method displayed by the application should be checked before using it for a contractual or legal purpose.

16. GST Calculator

Khata Bahi provides GST-related calculation functionality.

The user may enter:

  • Total bill amount
  • GST slab

The tool can calculate the applicable tax breakdown.

Users should remember that a calculator does not determine whether the chosen GST rate is legally applicable to a particular transaction.

17. CGST and SGST

For an applicable intra-state transaction, GST may involve CGST and SGST components.

A GST splitter can help calculate the components from a GST-inclusive amount.

Users should independently verify the applicable tax treatment.

18. Creating Invoice Information

The company profile may contain information used when generating invoice-style documents.

Keep the following information accurate:

  • Business name
  • GSTIN
  • Phone number
  • Address

Incorrect company information may result in incorrect documents.

19. Customer Information

When entering customer information, use accurate information where appropriate.

Do not enter unnecessary personal information.

Users should also follow applicable privacy and data-protection requirements when maintaining customer records.

20. Using Google Login

Where Google Login is available, select the login option and follow the authentication process.

Never provide your Google password directly to Khata Bahi.

Authentication should occur through the appropriate Google authentication interface.

After signing in, verify that the correct account is being used.

21. Synchronization

If synchronization is enabled for your account, ledger information may be synchronized through supported cloud infrastructure.

Synchronization can make account-based data management more convenient.

Nevertheless, users should maintain independent backups of important financial records.

22. Free Plan and Pro Plan

Khata Bahi may offer both free and paid functionality.

The current website displays:

Free Plan and a Pro option displayed at: ₹99 / 30 days.

Available features may change.

Always check the current application before purchasing.

23. UPI Payment

If purchasing Pro functionality through UPI:

  1. Open the Pro membership section.
  2. Review the displayed amount.
  3. Complete the payment through your UPI application.
  4. Keep the transaction reference/UTR.
  5. Enter the requested reference if the application asks for it.
  6. Submit the information.
  7. Wait for verification.

Do not disclose your UPI PIN to Khata Bahi or to anyone claiming to be support.

24. Protecting Your Data

Good bookkeeping requires good security practices.

Users should:

  • Use a secure Google account
  • Enable two-factor authentication where available
  • Lock their phone/computer
  • Avoid using shared devices for sensitive information
  • Keep backups
  • Check account activity
  • Avoid sharing passwords
  • Keep exported records secure

25. Correcting Errors

If you notice an incorrect transaction, review the relevant entry and correct it using the functionality available in the current version.

If the application does not provide the required correction facility, retain the original information and contact support.

Never intentionally alter records to create misleading financial information.

26. Regular Backups

A bookkeeping system should never be your only copy of important financial records.

Consider periodically exporting important information.

Store backups securely.

For especially important records, consider maintaining more than one backup location.

27. Using Khata Bahi with an Accountant

Khata Bahi can be used as a source of organized transaction information for discussion with an accountant.

You may export relevant records and provide them to your accountant.

Your accountant can then review the information and determine the appropriate accounting and tax treatment.

28. What Khata Bahi Does Not Replace

Khata Bahi does not replace:

  • A Chartered Accountant
  • A tax professional
  • A lawyer
  • A statutory auditor
  • Professional financial advice
  • Official government filing portals

Use the application as a digital business utility.

29. Troubleshooting

If the application does not behave as expected:

First check your internet connection.

Then:

  • Refresh the page.
  • Confirm that you are using a modern browser.
  • Confirm that you are signed into the correct account.
  • Check the active company.
  • Try the operation again.
  • Check whether the issue is limited to a particular file.
  • Keep a copy of any error message.
  • Contact support if the problem continues.

31. Final Best Practices

For reliable use of Khata Bahi:

  • Enter records regularly: Do not allow transactions to accumulate for months before recording them.
  • Check every amount: A single incorrect amount can affect totals.
  • Check the active company: This is particularly important if you use multiple businesses.
  • Back up important information: Do not depend on one digital copy.
  • Verify GST: Use the calculator as a calculation aid and confirm the applicable tax treatment.
  • Review invoices: Check business details before issuing documents.
  • Protect your account: Use strong account-security practices.
  • Keep professional advice separate: Use Khata Bahi for bookkeeping assistance, while relying on qualified professionals for matters requiring professional accounting, tax or legal judgment.
